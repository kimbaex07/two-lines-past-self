## Why the warning lands

The warning names one concrete, picturable moment: a specific night, a specific bus, a specific hospital room. It is not a general life lesson — it is a decision made in a single evening that could not be undone. The second line does not repeat the warning; it traces the consequence: that night was the last chance to hear those words, and the words became the standard every later decision was measured against. Two sentences, cause and effect, with nothing wasted — and the warning only makes sense because the reason gives it its weight.
